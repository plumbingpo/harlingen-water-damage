Kitchens: Leaks from a dishwasher, an ice maker line or the plumbing under the kitchen sink should be dealt with the day you find them. Crawlspaces: Dampness in the space below the floor can wait a few days, though not a few months. Mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a material getting wet, so a leak from months ago had plenty of time.
